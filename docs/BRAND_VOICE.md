# Get Local Hawaiʻi brand voice

Read this before writing any page, email, social post, asset or UI label for the site. Decisions behind it are in docs/DECISIONS.md (Oct 30).

## Who it is for and what it answers
- Oʻahu residents first, visitors second. Most arrive from search with a practical, right-now question about one business or market: is it open, when is the next market, where do I park.
- Every page answers "where and when" before anything else.

## Voice
- Plain, local, useful. Short sentences. Contractions. The site speaks as "I".
- Anonymous: never name or picture the author. No "born and raised", "as a local", bylines or personal stories.
- Say what a reader can do: "See if it's open before you go."
- Headlines read like something painted on a roadside sign: short, plain, all caps in Bungee.

## Honesty rules
- Listings come from public sources. "Checked" means a source was read on that date.
- Never narrate how the site works behind the scenes: no "I called", "I visited", "I check it against", "I review", "before it goes on the site". State the fact the reader needs and stop.
- Never claim completeness: no "every", "all", "full list", "complete" as a coverage claim.
- Status states only what a source posts. "Open now" only from posted hours. Missing facts say "not posted yet", never a guess.
- Attribute third-party claims ("calls itself", "per the Star-Advertiser") and tie relative claims to a year ("through 2025", not "the last 13 years").
- Generated photos are never captioned as a named, real market.
- No invented prices, counts, dates or promises. Use [PLACEHOLDER] in drafts.

## Patterns
- Home hero: WHAT'S OPEN ON OʻAHU / See if it's open before you go. Each listing shows the day I last checked its source.
- Headings say what is under them: WHERE TO FIND THEM, WHAT THEY SELL, WHO SELLS HERE. No mood headings (Good to know, At the markets).
- Status chips, caps: OPEN TIL 6P, STARTS 4P, NEXT: SUN NOV 1, HOURS NOT POSTED.
- Times: caps on chips (7:30A), lowercase in body (7:30a to 11a). Ranges use "to", never a dash.
- One honesty note per page, above the list, not on every row. One "Something changed? Tell me" per page.
- Claim prompts name two benefits, not three: "Claim Local Iʻa to post your market days and keep your hours current."
- Empty states say what is missing and offer "Tell me". Hide empty "more nearby" blocks.
- Photo captions name where the tile leads ("Farmers markets"), not a mood.
- Footer: I list what organizers and businesses post, plus other public sources. Each listing shows the day I last checked its source.

## Words
- Use: CHECKED [DATE], NEXT, NOT POSTED, WHERE TO FIND THEM, TELL ME, side names (Town, East, Windward, North Shore, Central, Leeward and ʻEwa).
- Never: shop local, support local, verified, confirmed, every or all (coverage), called, visited or stopped by, discover, explore, curated, hidden gem, vibrant, nestled, your guide to.
- No AI cadence: no lists of three for rhythm, no "not just X, it's Y", no "whether you're", no exclamation marks.

## Mechanics
- Never use em dashes in anything user-facing, commit messages or docs.
- Keep Hawaiian spelling exactly in copy: Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi, Kakaʻako, Waiʻanae, Kāneʻohe, Mānoa, Kaimukī, lūʻau. Titles, URLs and the schema name use plain spellings (Get Local Hawaii).
- Business names are written the way the business writes them.

## Visual quick reference
- Look: roadside stand signs. Arrow boards, price tags, hanging logo sign, nail heads, slight tilts. Character comes from shape, not heavy shadows.
- Type: Bungee (headlines, labels, times), Figtree (reading text), Playwrite NG Modern (logo and handwritten captions only).
- Color (dusty plum): ground #4A3354, band #3F2A48, footer #2A1C30, cream boards #FBF7F0, ink #221A26, muted #5F5766, brand purple #6B3A7D, secondary text on ground #DCCFE1, marigold #F2C14E on at most two items per screen, open green #2E6248.
- Art: painted island map (public/art/oahu-map.png, sides in src/data/oahu-sides.json) and the brush icon set (public/art/icons). Photos sit in taped snapshot frames.
